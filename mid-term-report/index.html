Mid-Term Report

Also known as Interim routine inspection, we can visit the property during the tenancy to highlight what condition the property is being kept in. This is crucial to make sure tenants are settling well and also gives you peace of mind that your assets are being looked after properly and there are no breaches to the tenancy agreement. At JNI Inventories we do monthly checks, 6 month checks or yearly checks. Mid-terms for rental properties are beneficial for both landlords and tenants and are the perfect opportunity to spot any problems with the property I.e making sure there is no subletting, negligence, maintenance issues, illegal activity etc.
