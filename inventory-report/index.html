Inventory Report

A thorough, impartial and detailed record of your property — delivered digitally within 48 hours.

Our inventory report is straightforward and to the point, detailing the property, furniture, fixtures and fittings provided in your home — backed with professional digital images taken and timestamped at the time of the inspection.

Having an inventory can help prevent disputes about the deposit at the end of the tenancy. We offer a 100% impartial and unbiased service and will record in great detail the conditions of the home at the time of inspection. Both the agent and tenant receive an instant copy the moment the report is signed.
